AirGlide
AIRGLIDE · WINDOWS COMPANION

Your phone.
Your pointer.

Move your Android phone or iPhone to move the Windows cursor. Click, scroll, type, and control media from your phone.

Installable Android and iPhone Home Screen app + portable Windows companion.
No Mac, Apple developer account, or app store installation needed.

1 Start on Windows

Extract the entire ZIP. Open the extracted folder and double-click START.cmd. Keep that window running. A setup page opens on your PC.

Both your phone and PC must use the same home network. Your PC can use Ethernet.

Android setup

Scan the Pair a phone QR code with your Android phone. Open in Chrome and choose Android. Download the PC certificate and follow the CA certificate installation instructions on the page. Open AirGlide, enter the PC code and tap Enable motion. In Chrome’s menu, choose Install app or Add to Home screen. Touchpad mode is available on phones without a usable motion sensor.

2 Scan with your iPhone

Use the iPhone Camera to scan the Pair a phone QR code on your PC. Open the page in Safari.

Follow its certificate setup steps. Safari needs this trusted local HTTPS connection before it can provide motion readings.

  1. Download the certificate profile from your own PC.
  2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install.
  3. Go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings.
  4. Enable trust for the matching AirGlide Local certificate.

The profile contains only a certificate. It adds no device management or VPN. Compare its fingerprint with the PC dashboard and remove the profile when you stop using AirGlide.

3 Pair and add to Home Screen

Tap Open AirGlide, then enter the six-digit code from your PC. Choose Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen, enabling Open as Web App if shown.

Open the new icon. Enter the PC code again if asked. Tap Enable motion and allow motion access.

4 Point and move

Hold your phone in portrait and aim its top edge toward your screen. Move your wrist gently. Tap the large Click button to select.

Hold reposition while adjusting your grip. Settings has pointer speed, smoothing, and direction controls. Keep the phone app visible while controlling the PC.

Having trouble connecting?

Keep the companion running, check the same home Wi-Fi, and use the latest QR code. If Windows firewall blocks the app, mark your trusted home network Private and run Enable-Private-Network.cmd as administrator. This optional helper allows only AirGlide on the private local network.
